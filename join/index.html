Join

We are recruiting PhD students and interns at MBZUAI to build trustworthy AI, bringing together autonomous agents, software engineering, formal methods and security.

Who fits

People who enjoy both rigorous reasoning and hands-on systems building. You do not need every background on that list; the best projects usually sit between two of them.

A practical filter: if you have ever been more interested in why a benchmark number was wrong than in improving it, you will like it here.

How to apply

PhD admission runs through the MBZUAI graduate programme, which is fully funded; name OASIS in your statement.

Either way, email {{ site.contact.email }} with your CV and a short note on what you would like to work on.
